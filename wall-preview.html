Wall theme — frame, not fill

Land of sun, sea, and stone

The polygonal wall is now used as architecture — present in the hero, the section dividers, the page edges, and the footer. Everywhere content lives, the background is plain limestone so images, maps, and text read cleanly.

Photography "set into the stone"

✦

Every image lives in a limestone frame with a mortar-coloured border and a soft inset shadow, so it sits inside the wall like a niche rather than floating on top of it. The body background is plain — no texture competing with photographs.

Atmospheric section

Where the wall is allowed to breathe back through

Sections marked .atmosphere bring the wall back at low intensity — useful for pull quotes, chapter intros, and other "between content" moments. Photos and maps never appear here.

The hero, the dividers, the footer, the page edges, and atmospheric sections — those are the only places the polygonal wall shows up.
