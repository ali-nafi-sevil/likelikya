Pinara

Πίναρα · Pinale — Minare köyü, Muğla province
At a glance. A major Lycian city carved into the base of a spectacular round cliff, held three votes in the Lycian League. Quiet, overgrown, and rarely crowded.

The cliff

The sheer round limestone cliff above Pinara — locally called the "honeycomb" — is pocked with hundreds of empty rectangular niches. Most are unfinished or symbolic tombs; reaching the actual interior of the cliff would have required scaffolding now long gone.

What to see

  • Theatre in a wooded gully — small, perfect, almost intact.
  • Royal Tomb with a relief showing a fortified city, possibly an image of Pinara itself.
  • Odeon and agora area, partly excavated.
  • The lower city, scattered through olive groves, where you can wander freely.

Pinara is one of the best Lycian sites for solitude. Bring water and good shoes — the paths are rough.
