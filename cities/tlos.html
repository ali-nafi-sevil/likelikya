Tlos

Τλώς · Tlawa — Yaka köyü, Muğla province
At a glance. One of the six leading cities of the Lycian League with three votes, called by 19th-century travellers "the brightest jewel of Lycia." Continuously inhabited for almost four millennia — Bronze Age, Hittite, Lycian, Greek, Roman, Byzantine, Ottoman.

The acropolis

A natural rock outcrop crowned by a Lycian fortress, refortified by the Ottoman bandit-prince Kanlı Ali Ağa in the 18th century. The view from the top covers the entire Eşen valley and the Akdağlar.

Bellerophon's tomb

On the east face of the acropolis: a temple-tomb with a famous relief of the hero Bellerophon on his winged horse Pegasus, lance raised against the Chimera. The Bellerophon myth was strongly localised in Lycia — the Chimera fire above Olympos was identified with the monster he defeated.

What else to see

  • Theatre — large, with elaborate carved cornices.
  • Roman baths with seven arched windows looking out over the valley.
  • Stadium and agora.
  • Byzantine basilica and necropolis.

Tlos pairs naturally with a trip to the nearby Saklıkent Gorge — one of the deepest canyons in the world, wadable in summer.
