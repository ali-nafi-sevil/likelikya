Phaselis

Φασηλίς — Kemer, Antalya province
At a glance. A wealthy ancient port founded around 690 BC by colonists from Rhodes, on a small peninsula with three natural harbours. Welcomed Alexander the Great with a golden crown; later became Roman.

The three harbours

Phaselis's signature feature: three separate bays, one on each side of the small peninsula and one to the north. The main paved street runs between two of them, lined with the agora and shop foundations. You can swim in any of the three.

What to see

  • Hadrian's Gate at the south end of the main street, AD 130.
  • Roman theatre against the slope of the acropolis.
  • Aqueduct running into the city across the marsh.
  • Necropolis with both sarcophagus and house-tomb types.
  • Tahtalı in the background — Phaselis sits at the foot of the 2,365 m mountain, accessible by cable car from nearby.

Phaselis is in a national park with an entry fee. The swimming beaches are good and the site is a manageable half-day combined with a swim.
