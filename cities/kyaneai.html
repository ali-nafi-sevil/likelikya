Kyaneai

Κυανέαι — Yavu, above Demre, Antalya province
At a glance. "The dark blue ones" — a small Lycian city on a long limestone ridge above the modern village of Yavu, holding the densest concentration of sarcophagi anywhere in Lycia.

The stone forest

Kyaneai's most striking feature is its necropolis: hundreds of Lycian sarcophagi crowd the ridge approach to the city, leaning at every angle, some still capped with their original Gothic-arched lids, some thrown open. Walking the path in feels like passing through a forest of stone.

The city itself

  • Theatre well-preserved, with arcaded vaults beneath the seating.
  • Agora and bouleuterion.
  • Hellenistic city walls partly intact.
  • Cisterns for water storage — like Termessos, no river.
  • Roman houses and Byzantine basilica.

A 45-minute climb up from Yavu. Very quiet. Bring water.
