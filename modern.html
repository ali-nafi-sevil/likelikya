Modern Lycia

Towns, food, and the seasons of the Teke coast

Lycia is also a place where people live today — farmers in the Xanthos valley, fishermen in Kaleköy, scuba instructors in Kaş, sea-kayak guides in Üçağız, pastoralists in the Akdağlar yaylas. This section is a slowly growing portrait of the modern coast.

The main towns

Coast · ~8,000 residents

Kaş

The most walkable of the Lycian towns. Whitewashed Old Town, the best diving on the coast, and a small theatre 200 m from the harbour.

Coast · ~3,500 residents

Kalkan

Steeply terraced over its harbour, more upmarket than Kaş, famous for sunset rooftops and the gateway to Patara beach.

Coast · district capital

Fethiye

Modern Telmessos, a busy regional centre with a Tuesday market, a deep harbour, and the rock-cut tombs of Amyntas above it.

Coast · ancient Myra

Demre

The agricultural heart of central Lycia, a sea of plastic greenhouses surrounding the Church of Saint Nicholas and the ruins of Myra.

Coast · car-free beach village

Çıralı & Olympos

Two adjacent villages at the foot of the Chimaera, kept low-rise by turtle-nesting regulation. Treehouses, river, and the Lycian Way passing through.

Inland · Xanthos valley

Kınık & Eşen

Agricultural villages on the river that fed ancient Xanthos. Citrus, pomegranate, and the country's main producer of greenhouse tomatoes.

Eat

  • Mezeler — small plates: charred eggplant, broad-bean purée, haydari, samphire, octopus salad, fried mussels.
  • Çöp şiş — tiny lamb skewers, an Antalya-region specialty.
  • Likya peyniri — a goat-cheese style local to the Teke peninsula, lightly aged in olive oil.
  • Kekik — wild thyme, used on almost every grilled fish.
  • Tahin pekmez — sesame paste with grape molasses, breakfast.

Activities

  • Sea-kayaking the sunken city — the Üçağız → Kaleköy → Kekova island day-paddle.
  • Diving Kaş — flying gurnards, octopus, wreck dives, sea caves; year-round.
  • Tandem paragliding off Babadağ — 1,960 m, classic Fethiye experience.
  • Tahtalı cable car — to 2,365 m in under ten minutes; panorama over the entire eastern coast.
  • Day-boats to Meis — from Kaş, EU passport required; a different country for lunch.
  • Lycian Way day-sections — even one or two days of the trail are worth the effort.

The year

March–April wildflowers, almond blossom, the trail at its prettiest. May–June swimming begins, festivals start. July–August hot and crowded; head for elevation or stay in the water. September–October the best months — warm sea, fewer people, ripening pomegranate and quince. November–February mild, often rainy, low-season prices; the inland sites are at their most atmospheric.

Events, tour operators, and seasonal calendars will be added here as the site grows.
