About likelikya

A working draft — built by hand, growing slowly

likelikya is a slow guide to Lycia — the southwestern coast of Turkey once home to the Lycian League, then the Romans, then the Byzantines, and still home today to a string of small towns, fishermen, farmers, and a 660-kilometre stretch of one of the world's most underrated coastlines.

What it covers

The ancient cities of Lycia and her near-neighbours; the modern Lycian Way trail; the ecology of the coast and mountains; the modern towns built on top of the ancient ones; and the small things — facts, anecdotes, anchor histories — that make a place actually interesting rather than just photogenic.

How it's built

By hand. The site is plain static HTML — no JavaScript framework, no tracking, no cookies. Pages are written one at a time. The intention is the opposite of a guidebook with 400 listings: fewer pages, better pages.

Contribute

If you have walked the trail, dug at a site, or live in one of the towns and have something to add — particularly small, specific, true things — you are very welcome to write in. (Contribution channel to be added when the site goes live.)

A note on scope

Lycia is the first region this site covers in detail because it is the one its author knows best. Other regions of Turkey may follow — possibly as separate sister sites, possibly under one growing index — but only after Lycia itself has been treated properly.
