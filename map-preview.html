Wall theme · interactive map

Lycia, on a map

Real OpenStreetMap tiles, real coordinates, real markers — every city from the site, click-through to the detail page.

Pan and zoom freely. Click any marker for a popup with a quick read on the city; click "Read more" to open its page. Markers are color-coded by geography: blue for coastal cities, cream for inland, tan for highland.

Tiles courtesy of OpenStreetMap contributors. Map library: Leaflet.

How this is built. Plain Leaflet via CDN, ~50 lines of JavaScript at the bottom of this file. The data lives in a single CITIES array — adding new sites (e.g. Limyra, Arycanda, Myra) is a matter of pasting in a row of [name, lat, lng, slug, ancient, geo, blurb].
